A cafe made for students

Founded in 2026 by former students, StudySip was created to give students a quiet, affordable place to study and eat in Braamfontein.

Our mission

To provide students with an affordable, comfortable and productive space to study, connect and refuel.

House rules

  • Ask our team for the free Wi-Fi password.
  • There may be a four-hour table limit during exam season.
  • Please keep the quiet zone peaceful after 18:00.
